How we learn together

Our pedagogy

  • Live coding: we learn by doing together, and mistakes are teaching moments, not detours.
  • Mental models: new skills land when we connect them to the work you already do.
  • No overload: a few key concepts done well beats racing through everything.

Managing expectations

  • Practical competence: skills you can use in your role right away, not exhaustive coverage.
  • Collaborative pace: we move as a group, and your questions help set the speed.
  • Mistakes are expected: coding is troubleshooting and persistence, for all of us.
