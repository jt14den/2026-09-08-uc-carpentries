How we learn together

Our pedagogy

  • Live coding: we learn by doing together, and mistakes are teaching moments, not detours.
  • Mental models: new skills land when we connect them to the work you already do.
  • No overload: a few key concepts done well beats racing through everything.

Managing expectations

  • Practical competence: skills you can use in your role right away, not exhaustive coverage.
  • Collaborative pace: we move as a group, and your questions help set the speed.
  • Mistakes are expected: coding is troubleshooting and persistence, for all of us.

Using Zoom reactions

Green check = “I've got it.”   Red X = “I'm stuck” — a helper will DM you.
Actually do this live: share your screen, hover the Reactions button, open the tray, and click the green check so everyone sees it appear on your video tile. Ask the whole room to send a green check right now as a test — it tells you the signal is working and warms them up to using it. Then: during exercises, green check when you're done, red X if you're stuck and a helper will reach out. Slower / faster are always welcome. Icons may sit under “More” depending on the Zoom build and whether the host enabled non-verbal feedback — check yours before class.
